# Drift log

What the **live portal** does differently from
[process-steps.md](process-steps.md).

Fill this in as you re-map. It is how the team learns what the carrier changed,
and it is what stops `process-steps.md` quietly rotting into fiction.

**An empty drift log after re-mapping a week-old runbook against a live portal
means the re-mapping did not happen.** Portals change; record it.

## How to add an entry

| Date | Documented step | What the portal actually does | What the automation does now |
|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | 2.2.2 "Type the producer first name into broker search" | Field is now a typeahead requiring 3+ characters; results render in a listbox, not a dropdown | Fills the first name, waits for `role=listbox`, clicks the option by full name |

Then correct `process-steps.md` and `field-map.md`, marking the corrected lines
so the diff against the original stays visible.
